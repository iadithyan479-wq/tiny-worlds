A FIELD GUIDE FOR CURIOUS OBSERVERS

Look closer. Change one thing.

Tiny Worlds is a simplified living system, not a scripted story. Plants respond to warmth and rain; every animal has needs; descendants carry their parents' traits, with small differences.

01 / WATCH

Follow the food web

Bees and mice use meadow plants. Frogs hunt bees. Foxes and owls hunt mice and frogs. More life doesn't always mean a healthier world.

02 / NOTICE

Read the small signs

Choose an animal to see its energy, health, ancestry, and inherited traits. Plain-language notes connect today's conditions to what it needs.

03 / NUDGE

Change a condition

Adjust warmth, rain, habitat, or fertility. Try a brief disturbance and use the history scrubber to trace the ripple afterward.

04 / RETURN

Save your field journal

Named worlds are stored only in this browser. Export a JSON file for a portable backup; importing another world replaces the current one after you confirm.
